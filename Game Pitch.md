# Game Pitch: _Granite Town_ (Working Title)

## 1. Logline

An artisan-adventure game set in 1880s New Brunswick. Play as a stonecutting father and his adventurous daughter as you build a beautiful town, brave the dangers of the industrial wild, and save your family's home from a ruthless corporate rival.

## 2. Game Overview & Core Vibe

_Granite Town_ is a 2D, top-down "cozy" game that blends creative expression and town beautification with a heartfelt story of exploration, risk, and community.

Instead of a high-stress management sim or a combat-heavy RPG, this is a **dual-protagonist adventure** where you "tag-team" between two characters whose goals are intertwined:

- **The Parent:** An artisan stonecutter. Their gameplay is **creative, crafty, and nurturing.** You don't "grind" for rocks; you manage a **passive supply** of stone to beautify the town, manage the household budget, and nurture your child.
    
- **The Child:** An explorer and adventurer. Their gameplay is **curious, physical, and self-sufficient.** She explores lost ruins, solves environmental puzzles with her **Slingshot**, and maintains her own gear to survive the wild.
    

The "Risky Play" of Yesteryear:

The game captures the freedom and danger of 1880s childhood. The "granite industry" is a playground. Scrapes, bruises, and ruined clothes are part of life. Managing your health ("Grit") is a resource; you might deliberately take a tumble to reach a secret area, but push too hard, and you’ll be grounded with an injury.

## 3. Setting & Story

- **Where:** St. George, New Brunswick ("Granite Town").
    
- **When:** The game begins in **Spring, 1881**.
    
- **The Backstory:** The town is recovering from the bankruptcy of the _Bay of Fundy Red Granite Co._.
    
- **The Antagonist:** A ruthless American manager who bought the old company's assets, including the massive finishing mill at **Magaguadavic Falls**.
    
- **The Central Plot:** The Child's secret clubhouse is near the "Lake Utopia Stone Medallion" site. The Antagonist believes this site hides a "motherlode" of red granite and will destroy the sanctuary to get it.
    

## 4. The Core Gameplay Loops

### The Child: The Adventurer (Exploration, Risk & Tools)

The Child's gameplay is about exploring, discovering, and taking responsibility for her own survival.

- **"Grit" (Health as a Resource):** The Child possesses a "Grit" meter representing stamina. You can bypass puzzles by taking physical risks, but if Grit hits zero, you sustain an injury (sprained ankle) and must enter a "Recovery Phase," restricting you to town social quests.
    
- **The Slingshot (Cozy Danger):** The Child's primary tool. It is not a weapon for killing, but for interaction:
    
    - **Solve Puzzles:** Hit a distant lever to drop a bridge.
        
    - **Create Distractions:** Hit a metal sheet in the mill to draw a guard away.
        
    - **Fend off Nuisances:** Scare off a coyote or dispatch a rat blocking the path.
        
- **"Threads & Treads" (Gear Durability):** Clothing acts as armor.
    
    - **Action-Based Wear:** Running wears down boots; crawling tears clothes.
        
    - **The Mending Kit (Self-Care):** The Child is responsible for her own gear. When clothes get "Ragged," she must play a mending minigame to fix them.
        
- **Adventure Zones ("Dungeons"):**
    
    - **The Abandoned Quarries:** Flooded ruins and environmental puzzle boxes.
        
    - **The Rival's Mill:** A stealth-puzzle zone at **Magaguadavic Falls** focused on pattern recognition.
        
    - **The Letete Mines:** Abandoned **19th-century copper mine shafts** requiring light and durable gear.
        

---

### The Parent: The Artisan (Crafting, Creation & Finance)

The Parent's gameplay is about creative expression and keeping the family afloat.

- **Passive Material Supply (Anti-Grind):** The workshop receives a **passive daily income** of Rough Stone based on how many quarries the Child has discovered. This removes the grind of harvesting, allowing focus on crafting.
    
- **The Household Budget:** While stone is free, money is scarce. You must balance buying expensive tools against saving for emergencies (Medicine/Doctors) if the Child gets hurt.
    
- **The Monument Designer:** A detailed creative loop where villagers request memorials. You choose:
    
    1. **Stone Type** (Red, Black, Grey).
        
    2. **Shape** (Column, Obelisk, Slab).
        
    3. **Finish** (Polished or Hammered).
        
    4. **Engraving** (Pixel-art anchor, flower, etc.).
        
- **The "Memories" Collection:** Refurbishing "Lost Items" found by the Child (e.g., a _Tattered Sheet of Music_) to display in the home.
    

---

### The Design Studio (Shared Creative Loop)

Located in the family home, this station allows for **Clothing Design.**

- **Pattern Making:** The player can design clothes, choosing Fabric, Cut, and Detail.
    
- **"Breeches & Boy Clothes":** The player can craft trousers for the Child.
    
    - **Benefit:** Higher durability and climbing speed.
        
    - **Social Consequence:** Some NPCs may disapprove, while workers may offer respect or access to industrial zones.
        

## 5. The Synergistic Loop (How They Work Together)

1. **Child Finds Problem:** The Child finds a chasm blocking the path.
    
2. **Social Puzzles (The Ruffians):** "Ruffians" block a path (class tension). They cannot be fought. The Child asks the Parent to **craft** a "Warm Meal" or "New Coat" to give to them, turning rivals into friends.
    
3. **Parent Gets Project:** The Parent receives a project: "Repair the Old Derrick."
    
4. **Risk vs. Wallet:** The Parent needs money for parts. If the Child gets injured rushing to find resources, the Parent must spend "Derrick Money" on a Doctor, delaying the project.
    
5. **World Unlocks:** Once the project is done, the Child can access the new area.
    

## 6. Key Features & Progression

- **A Living, Dangerous World (Seasons):**
    
    - **Spring/Summer:** High water levels block paths. Exporting goods is slow, relying on the **Schooner** at the wharf.
        
    - **Autumn/Winter:** Water freezes, creating "Ice Paths" but introducing "The Chill" (Exposure/Sickness).
        
- **The First Winter Event:** The completion of the **Grand Southern Railway** coincides with a massive blizzard.
    
    - **Rescue Mission:** Parent and Child must survive the storm.
        
    - **Economic Upgrade:** The railway permanently unlocks **St. George Station**, providing a faster, reliable way to ship monuments and fund projects.
        
- **The Sketchbook:** A collection log of local flora, history, and the Lake Utopia Monster.
    

## 7. Development Vision

- **Scope:** An achievable 6/10 difficulty project.
    
- **The 1-Year Goal:** Early Access build featuring the main story, seasonal cycle, and the core Parent/Child loop.
